Proportional Impact  ·  Gun Deaths Relative to Population

Compare each group's share of the population against their share of gun deaths, and see the rate per 100k of their own population

Note on small numbers: CDC WONDER suppresses any state/year/group cell with fewer than 10 deaths, and this chart is built from national totals aggregated from those state-level cells — so a handful of very small, suppressed cells are folded in as unreported rather than fabricated. Bars driven by 20 or fewer deaths (CDC's own "unreliable rate" threshold) are shown lighter with a dashed edge in the bottom chart.
