US Firearm Deaths — Deaths per 100,000 Residents, by Race, by State

Rate is scaled to each race group's own population in that state  ·  Sorted highest → lowest  ·  1999–2024

Reading small numbers: CDC WONDER suppresses any state/year/race/sex cell with fewer than 10 deaths, so a missing state for a given race/year means its count was suppressed (or genuinely zero) — not necessarily zero. Bars built on 20 or fewer deaths (CDC's own "unreliable rate" threshold) are shown lighter with a dashed edge; a small state can rank #1 on a handful of deaths, and that's a sampling artifact, not a real extreme.
